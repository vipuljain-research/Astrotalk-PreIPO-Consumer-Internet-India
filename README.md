Astrotalk — Pre-IPO Research (Consumer Internet)

Research Note (PDF)
An investment thesis on Astrotalk, India's largest online astrology marketplace, which reported FY26 operating revenue of ₹1,823 crore (+55%) and net profit of ₹222 crore, and became a $1B unicorn in August 2026. The note covers the business model, why customers choose Astrotalk over matrimony platforms like Shaadi.com, a peer comparison across Indian astrology apps, listed matrimony players and global psychic apps, new product levers, international expansion beyond the Hindu diaspora, and a Bear/Base/Bull FY28 valuation ahead of a planned IPO.

Verdict: Positive, pre-IPO. Track for the DRHP.

A rare Indian consumer internet company that is large, fast-growing and profitable at the same time. The open question is whether growth holds as ad spend falls and AI enters the category.
